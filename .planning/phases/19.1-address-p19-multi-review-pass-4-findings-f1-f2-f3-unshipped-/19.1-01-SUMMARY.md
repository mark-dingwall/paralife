### Task 01b — F1: composite formation uses remapEntity (D-02)

Two-line replacement in `SimulationEngine.updateBotRegistryForFormation:887-889`: removed the buggy `unregisterByEntity(bp.id()) + register(sessionId, newMemberId, pos)` sequence and replaced with `remapEntity(sessionId, newMemberId, pos)`, mirroring the composite-revert site at `:1258`.

**Bug root:** `unregisterByEntity` always enqueues a `DeathNotice` (BotRegistry:113), causing `TickBroadcaster` to emit phantom `v|D` terminal frames to live sessions after composite formation.

**Test design note:** `TickBroadcaster @Order(50)` drains `botRegistry.drainDeaths()` within the same `publishEvent` call chain, making post-tick `drainDeaths()` assertions unconditionally empty. The `@SpyBean BotRegistry` + `verify(botRegistry, never()).unregisterByEntity(BP1_ID)` approach is the correct RED-before-fix, GREEN-after-fix gate.

**Commit:** `80b5eef`

**Evidence:**
```

→ :732 (bond formation), :888 (composite formation NEW), :1258 (composite revert), :1318 (dissolve)
```

### Task 01c — F3: markDead clears resume token (D-03)

`WorldWebSocketHandler.markDead` now mirrors `cleanupBot:847-858`: reads `entityId` before remove, also removes `ATTR_RESUME_TOKEN`, and calls `resumeTokenRegistry.clearActive(entityId)`.

**Bug root:** `markDead` only removed `ATTR_ENTITY_ID`. Every solo death leaked an ACTIVE entry in `ResumeTokenRegistry.tokenMap` forever.
