## Push-back rationale — D1 (Gemini cleanupBot thread-safety)

Gemini flagged HIGH on cleanupBot's writes to `BuffRegistry.byEntity`, `EnvCleanupHooksBean.infections`, and `EnvironmentEngine.fleeing` from cross-thread cleanup paths.

**Verified live:** all three are `ConcurrentHashMap`-backed:

- `BuffRegistry.byEntity` — `private final ConcurrentHashMap<String, ...> byEntity` (verified by grep on `BuffRegistry.java`).
- `EnvCleanupHooksBean.infections` — `private final ConcurrentHashMap<String, ...> infections` (verified by grep on `EnvCleanupHooksBean.java`).
- `EnvironmentEngine.fleeing` — `private final ConcurrentHashMap<String, ...> fleeing` (verified by grep on `EnvironmentEngine.java`).

The "single-threaded simulation core" convention in `CLAUDE.md` covers grid-state mutations during the tick (`WorldGrid` writes, `Cell` mutations, occupant-bearing structures). Auxiliary registries listed above are explicitly concurrent for cross-thread cleanup — that's their purpose. Gemini's HIGH verdict treats the convention as broader than it is.

**Decision:** dismissed; no F-amendment needed. The convention text in CLAUDE.md is correct and the live code matches it.

## Downgrade rationale — C4 / D2 (E2.3 cure-event invariant)

Codex pass-5 correctly observed that E2.3's amendment text says "cure events do not flow through `pendingGrants`", but live source has `EnvironmentEngine.reduceInfection` (`:909`) calling `addPendingGrant(new PendingGrant(...))` on cure expiration AND `tickBuffsAndInfections` (`:744`) does the same. The factual claim was wrong.

**Conclusion still holds:** comparator totality argument survives because each cure produces at most one `PendingGrant` per `(entity, initialTicks, position)` tuple — `reduceInfection` early-returns when `infections.get(entityId) == null`, so a second cure for the same entity in the same tick is a no-op. The 4-key cascade `(entityId, initialTicks, position.x, position.y)` is still total in practice; it's just total via an empirical invariant rather than via the (false) "cures don't reach pendingGrants" path.

**Decision:** doc bug, not correctness gap. Restate E2.3 with the correct mechanism; keep the comparator + the "load-bearing ADR if invariant weakens" fallback. Captured under F4.

Same reasoning applies to D2 (Claude HIGH on D-04 invariant ADR): empirical impossibility holds, ADR not yet required, anti-reflag comment in `PendingGrant` source per F6 #1 makes the invariant visible to future readers.
