Restore the sensory-organ perception model (D-01/D-03/D-04) inside TickBroadcaster. Replace the flat `tierOf()` authority-tier dispatch with per-role frame routing: LOCOMOTOR gets its own 8-cell adjacency stitched with the union of all SENSOR members' 5x5 windows; FEEDER/ATTACKER get 8-cell adjacency only; REPRODUCER/DEFENDER/SENSOR are passive. Purpose: SENSOR members must produce tangible composite value (extended FOV feeding LOCOMOTOR voting) instead of acting passively. This is the foundational wire-shape change the rest of the phase builds on.

Output: Modified TickBroadcaster, updated CompositePerceptionTest, new SensorStitchedPerceptionTest. No wire/codec protocol change (MAX_S_ENTRIES=256, relative-coord ±63 are sufficient).
