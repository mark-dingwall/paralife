### Integration Gap

**PerceptionBroadcaster.buildPerception() selfState** — `PerceptionBroadcaster.java:94` only handles `instanceof Particle` for selfState construction. When a bot's Particle forms a BondedPair, the cell occupant becomes BondedPair, the `instanceof Particle` check fails, and the bot receives `UNKNOWN/0` selfState. The bot's `HeuristicBrain.decide()` catches `IllegalArgumentException` on `ParticleType.valueOf("UNKNOWN")` and permanently returns `Decision.rest()`. Bonded bots become zombie resters.

**ActionResolver entity check** — `ActionResolver.java:115-121` checks `cell.occupant() instanceof Particle` to confirm an entity is alive. BondedPair bots fail this check and get silently unregistered. BondedPairs have no action-submission path.

These are architectural decisions that phase 12 (composite movement) will need to address.
